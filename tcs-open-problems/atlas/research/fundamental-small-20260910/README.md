# Additional fundamental problems for the current small categories

Research date: 10 September 2026. This directory is an editorial proposal, not
newly published catalogue cards. It uses the 25 small categories in the current
`taxonomy.py` and `CATEGORY_PLAN.md`, including Structural graph theory and
Beyond worst-case and average-case analysis.

The user's hard requirement is fundamental importance; the requested 5–10 per
category is not a license to include routine extensions. Candidates must also
be absent from the entire catalogue, including differently classified records.

`baseline.json` preserves the 6,513-record snapshot used for novelty review.
`seeds.tsv` is brainstorming ONLY: it intentionally includes candidates later
rejected as duplicated, resolved, ill-posed, or insufficiently fundamental.
`novelty-screen.json` records lexical matches, separating question matches from
mere source-title context. Neither zero matches nor a keyword hit is by itself
a semantic novelty verdict.

Sources are primary research, author surveys, or author-maintained problem
lists. A paper's date is taken from its version history, not a search engine's
publication-age label. Claimed results and restricted-model results are checked
before marking a classical problem resolved.

Proposals are ranked editorially within each category by the breadth of their
consequences and centrality to the field. No score from the old bulk importance
pass is treated as evidence of mathematical significance or open status.
