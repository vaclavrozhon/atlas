## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Research direction:** Find useful closed-form optimal discrimination probabilities for ensembles of three or more states. — §3.1, introduction. [PDF p. 133](../pdf/watrous.pdf#page=133)

2. **Question:** Construct explicit quantum channels witnessing non-additivity of Holevo information; existence is established nonconstructively. — §8.5, discussion of non-additivity. [PDF p. 547](../pdf/watrous.pdf#page=547)
