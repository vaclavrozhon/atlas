## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Question:** Find a natural OutputP problem outside IncP, including the proposed K_t-free domination candidate. — Open problem 3.1. [PDF p. 22](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=22)

2. **Question:** Weaken or reverse the ETH assumption behind the strict incremental-time hierarchy. — Open problem 3.2. [PDF p. 23](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=23)

3. **Question:** Conditionally separate DelayP and IncP from their polynomial-space versions. — Open problem 4.1. [PDF p. 32](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=32)

4. **Question:** Separate memoryless NextP from DelayP, preferably even polynomial-space DelayP. — Open problem 4.2. [PDF p. 32](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=32)

5. **Question:** Enumerate binary-matroid circuits in incremental polynomial time and polynomial space. — Open problem 4.3. [PDF p. 33](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=33)

6. **Question:** Amortize unknown incremental delay while retaining polynomial space and polynomial delay. — Open problem 4.4. [PDF p. 37](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=37)

7. **Conjecture:** Exclude strong polynomial delay for DNF-model enumeration under a standard hypothesis. — Open problem 5.1; Conjecture 5.1.1. [PDF p. 41](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=41)

8. **Question:** Obtain sublinear-in-input-set-count average delay for union closure; settle strong polynomial delay. — Open problem 5.2; also p.61. [PDF p. 41](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=41)

9. **Question:** Conditionally separate strong polynomial delay from polynomial delay. — Open problem 5.3. [PDF p. 43](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=43)

10. **Question:** Characterize when constant amortized enumeration becomes constant worst-case delay. — Open problem 5.4. [PDF p. 45](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=45)

11. **Question:** Derive enumeration directly from random-walk or Boltzmann-sampling machinery. — Open problem 5.5. [PDF p. 47](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=47)

12. **Question:** Find a natural hardness benchmark for DelayP inside a higher incremental-time class. — Open problem 7.1. [PDF p. 54](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=54)

13. **Question:** Transfer hardness equivalences between strong-delay and incremental-time classes. — Open problem 7.2. [PDF p. 54](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=54)

14. **Question:** Replace SETH by ETH in strongly-accessible-set-system enumeration lower bounds. — Open problem 7.3. [PDF p. 54](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=54)

15. **Question:** Use SETH to exclude small delay for transversals, binary-matroid circuits or maximal cliques. — Open problem 7.4. [PDF p. 55](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=55)

16. **Question:** Prove unconditional constant-pebble JAG lower bounds for distance-two query enumeration. — Open problem 7.5. [PDF p. 58](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=58)

17. **Research direction:** Develop enumeration circuit classes, unconditional separations and parallel/descriptive-complexity connections. — Open problem 7.6. [PDF p. 58](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=58)

18. **Question:** Establish a finite-domain enumeration-CSP dichotomy. — Open problem 7.7. [PDF p. 59](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=59)

19. **Question:** Prove IncP hardness for uniform closure under input-specified clones. — Open problem 7.8. [PDF p. 61](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=61)

20. **Question:** Characterize supergraph enumeration for higher-arity clones and eliminate exponential space via reverse search. — Open problem 7.9. [PDF p. 61](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=61)

21. **Question:** Can one unary operator affecting two components make closure membership NP-hard? — Open problem 7.10. [PDF p. 62](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=62)

22. **Question:** Solve Mon-Factor for further polynomial classes; develop reverse-search polynomial-delay interpolation. — Open problem 7.11. [PDF p. 66](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=66)

23. **Question:** Compress previously enumerated monomials or interpolate with polynomial space. — Open problem 7.12; continuation p.67. [PDF p. 66](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=66)

24. **Question:** Obtain output-polynomial generation of molecular maps; bound the existing algorithm’s total time. — Open problem 8.1. [PDF p. 71](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=71)

25. **Question:** Characterize enumeration via product/union circuits and tractable join extensions. — Open problem 9.1. [PDF p. 77](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=77)

26. **Research direction:** Find useful solution decompositions beyond unions and Cartesian products. — Open problem 9.2. [PDF p. 77](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=77)

27. **Question:** Constant-factor under-approximate a natural problem not known to belong to IncP. — Open problem 9.3. [PDF p. 79](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=79)

28. **Question:** Efficiently cover spanning trees or shortest paths under meaningful solution distances. — Open problem 9.4. [PDF p. 80](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=80)

29. **Question:** Obtain distance-cover approximations through supergraph walks or flashlight search. — Open problem 9.5. [PDF p. 80](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=80)

30. **Question:** Generate r spanning trees maximizing minimum pairwise Hamming distance in polynomial time. — Open problem 9.6. [PDF p. 80](../pdf/enumeration_strozecki.pdf#page=80)
