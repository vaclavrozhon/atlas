                                                                         Abstract
         We study the local optimality of Gaussian distributions for the Han-Kobayashi inner bound of the Gaussian Z-interference
     channel. We identify parameters (a, P1 , P2 ) where Gaussian distributions do not optimize the Han-Kobayashi achievable region,
     as long as power control is not employed.

                                                             I. I NTRODUCTION
   In this paper, we study the scalar one-sided interference channel (or Z-interference) given by Y1 = X1 + Z1 and Y2 =
X2 + X1 + Z2 , as depicted in Figure 1. Here, X1 and X2 are transmitted signals constrained to have average powers P1 and
P2 , respectively, a ∈ (0, 1) is an interference gain, Z1 and Z2 are standard Gaussians, and Y1 and Y2 are the two received
signals. Thus, this Z-interference channel model is specified using three parameters (a, P1 , P2 ). We use the standard definition
of the capacity region, denoted by C, as considered in [1].

A. Han-Kobayashi achievable region
   The most well-known inner bound for the interference channel is the Han-Kobayashi achievable region. while there are
examples where the Han-Kobayashi inner bound is not equal to the capacity region [2], for certain special classes of interference
channels, the Han-Kobayashi inner bound has been shown to be optimal [3], [4]. It remains an open question whether the
Han-Kobayashi inner bound with Gaussian signaling and power control is tight for the Gaussian Z-interference channels.

attempt to understand the various claims made in [5]. Please see Remark 2 in this regard.
