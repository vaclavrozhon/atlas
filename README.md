# Atlas reader

Open [index.html](index.html) directly or run `make serve` from the repository root.
The reader supports search, categories, Compact and Full layouts, Top 100 and
Top 1000, public notes and proposed community problems. Shared contributions use
GitHub; the catalogue and local mathematical rendering work offline.

Top 100 takes the first 5/2 problems in each large/small category. Top 1000 takes
the first 50/20 from the same order. Focus choices balance importance and diversity;
remaining positions follow editorial importance scores. Unfilled and reserved
places remain explicit. Resolved and retired records are hidden from the reader.
See [benchmark-selection.md](benchmark-selection.md).

Selection inventory: 1,279 large-category and 1,470 small-category candidates; 4,490 deleted records (IDs and reasons only); 2,749 saved in total.

`index.html`, `app.js`, `community.js`, `style.css`, `.nojekyll` and `vendor/` are
reader assets. JSON, CSV, `data.js`, category and selection reports, summary
collections and live-update files are generated by `make publish`. Their source
is [data/](../data/README.md). Generated catalogue files are never input to the
next build; existing snapshots are read only to calculate live-update differences.

Deleted cards are absent from the catalogue and all current exports. Their IDs
and reasons are maintained in `../data/deleted_records.json`. The reader has no
archive view. Research sources and the independent library are outside this
web document root.

A dated source's open label does not establish current openness. Detailed cards,
source drafts and legacy index entries retain their evidence level and caveats.
A formulation review does not verify current open status or a formal proof.

Review progress: 248 completed reviews; 2,501 remaining. Completed reviews include 2 individually justified dispositions.
