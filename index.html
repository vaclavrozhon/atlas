About & downloads

The benchmark covers problems that were open on January 1, 2026, including those with a public solution since then. Each assessment gives the probability that a public solution to the stated problem exists. Below 10% we label it “Likely open,” above 90% “Public solution likely,” and in between “Uncertain.” For a solution, we record the earliest public date we found.
