A principal modern textbook on fixed-parameter algorithms and lower bounds.

## Reading scope

Selected unresolved algorithmic gaps and central conjectures across the text.

This is a source inventory. The entries describe what this version presents as unresolved. Current open status has not been checked. Summaries can group related variants and are not substitutes for the source definitions.

This background reference retains the original selective inventory; it is not part of the specialist reread for the 35 categories.

Historical questions are retained as posed; later developments are outside this source inventory.

## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Question:** Determine the complexity of computing treewidth exactly on planar graphs. — Chapter 7, branchwidth discussion. [PDF p. 245](../pdf/cygan2015.pdf#page=245)

2. **Question:** Does planar Minimum Bisection admit a polynomial-time algorithm? — Chapter 7 bibliographic notes. [PDF p. 259](../pdf/cygan2015.pdf#page=259)

3. **Question:** Compute chromatic number in O*(2ⁿ) time using only polynomial space. — §10.2. [PDF p. 343](../pdf/cygan2015.pdf#page=343)

4. **Question:** Construct linear representations of transversal matroids deterministically in polynomial time. — §12.1.4. [PDF p. 399](../pdf/cygan2015.pdf#page=399)

5. **Question:** Find matroid circuits of size at least k, parameterized by k, for integer representations. — §12.2. [PDF p. 402](../pdf/cygan2015.pdf#page=402)

6. **Question:** Separate levels of the W-hierarchy, including the Independent Set and Dominating Set levels. — §13.3. [PDF p. 451](../pdf/cygan2015.pdf#page=451)

7. **Question:** Determine fixed-parameter tractability of finding a small nonempty even set. — §13.5, Even Set. [PDF p. 472](../pdf/cygan2015.pdf#page=472)

8. **Conjecture:** Resolve the Exponential-Time Hypothesis and its strong version. — Conjectures 14.1–14.2. [PDF p. 485](../pdf/cygan2015.pdf#page=485)

9. **Question:** Remove the logarithmic loss from the stated ETH-based exponent lower bound. — Chapter 14, Subgraph Isomorphism. [PDF p. 505](../pdf/cygan2015.pdf#page=505)

10. **Conjecture:** Resolve the Set Cover Conjecture. — Conjecture 14.36. [PDF p. 523](../pdf/cygan2015.pdf#page=523)

11. **Question:** Separate polynomial compression from polynomial kernelization for natural problems. — §15.1.2. [PDF p. 549](../pdf/cygan2015.pdf#page=549)
