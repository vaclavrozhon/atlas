## Why included

A foundational textbook on Fourier methods for Boolean functions and their TCS applications.

## Reading scope

Selected substantive unresolved questions in the corrected 2021 edition.

This is a source inventory. The entries describe what this version presents as unresolved. Current open status has not been checked. Summaries can group related variants and are not substitutes for the source definitions.

This background reference retains the original selective inventory; it is not part of the specialist reread for the 35 categories.

The edition explicitly gives a counterexample to Majority Is Least Stable; that formulation is excluded.

## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Conjecture:** Is the Fourier spectrum of a small DNF concentrated on a correspondingly small set? — §4.1, Mansour’s conjecture. [PDF p. 96](../pdf/odonnell2021.pdf#page=96)

2. **Conjecture:** Does every linear threshold function have degree-at-most-one Fourier weight at least 2/π? — Conjecture 5.3. [PDF p. 115](../pdf/odonnell2021.pdf#page=115)

3. **Question:** Can growing-size juntas be learned from uniformly random examples in polynomial time? — §6.4. [PDF p. 156](../pdf/odonnell2021.pdf#page=156)

4. **Question:** Improve the error dependence when sums of independent small-bias distributions fool low-degree polynomials. — §6.5, after Viola’s theorem. [PDF p. 163](../pdf/odonnell2021.pdf#page=163)

5. **Question:** Determine the asymptotic randomized decision-tree complexity of recursive majority-of-three. — §8.4, Example 8.64. [PDF p. 231](../pdf/odonnell2021.pdf#page=231)

6. **Conjecture:** Must a bounded low-degree polynomial have an influence polynomially large in variance divided by degree? — §8.7, Aaronson–Ambainis conjecture. [PDF p. 246](../pdf/odonnell2021.pdf#page=246)

7. **Question:** Must some squared Fourier coefficient be at least exp(−O(total influence))? — §9.6. [PDF p. 270](../pdf/odonnell2021.pdf#page=270)

8. **Conjecture:** Approximate biased-measure monotone functions of bounded influence by bounded-width monotone DNFs. — §10.4, Friedgut’s conjecture. [PDF p. 305](../pdf/odonnell2021.pdf#page=305)
