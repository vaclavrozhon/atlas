## Reading scope

Selected explicit questions from Chapters 12 and 19; other chapters also contain open discussions.

This is a source inventory. The entries describe what this version presents as unresolved. Current open status has not been checked. Summaries can group related variants and are not substitutes for the source definitions.

This background reference retains the original selective inventory; it is not part of the specialist reread for the 35 categories.

These questions are historical to the 2007 volume. Multipart research directions remain grouped rather than becoming artificial separate problems.

## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Question:** Does truthful makespan scheduling on related machines admit a PTAS? — §12.2. [PDF p. 331](../pdf/algorithmic_game_theory.pdf#page=331)

2. **Question:** Obtain truthful constant-factor combinatorial-auction approximations, including submodular valuations. — §12.3. [PDF p. 337](../pdf/algorithmic_game_theory.pdf#page=337)

3. **Research direction:** Determine approximation limitations of deterministic dominant-strategy truthful auctions. — §12.3. [PDF p. 338](../pdf/algorithmic_game_theory.pdf#page=338)

4. **Question:** Characterize domains where affine maximizers are the only implementable allocation rules. — §12.4. [PDF p. 341](../pdf/algorithmic_game_theory.pdf#page=341)

5. **Question:** Can efficient algorithmic implementation approximate better than every efficient dominant-strategy implementation? — §12.5. [PDF p. 348](../pdf/algorithmic_game_theory.pdf#page=348)

6. **Question:** Bound the local connection game’s price of anarchy by a constant for all edge-cost parameters. — §19.2, Open Problems. [PDF p. 529](../pdf/algorithmic_game_theory.pdf#page=529)

7. **Research direction:** Extend network-formation efficiency bounds to heterogeneous costs, traffic and approximate equilibria. — §19.2, Open Problems. [PDF p. 529](../pdf/algorithmic_game_theory.pdf#page=529)

8. **Research direction:** Determine tight equilibrium-efficiency bounds for undirected global connection and generalized cost-sharing games. — §19.3, Open Problems. [PDF p. 531](../pdf/algorithmic_game_theory.pdf#page=531)

9. **Research direction:** Identify further pricing games with efficient equilibria and guarantees after limited play. — §19.4, Open Problems. [PDF p. 532](../pdf/algorithmic_game_theory.pdf#page=532)
