## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Question:** Does P equal NP? — §2.1. [PDF p. 57](../pdf/arora_barak.pdf#page=57)

2. **Question:** Does deterministic logspace suffice for directed reachability, equivalently L=NL? — §4.2. [PDF p. 95](../pdf/arora_barak.pdf#page=95)

3. **Question:** Is directed reachability solvable simultaneously in polynomial time and polylogarithmic space? — Chapter 4 exercises. [PDF p. 105](../pdf/arora_barak.pdf#page=105)

4. **Question:** Does NC equal P? — §6.5.2. [PDF p. 126](../pdf/arora_barak.pdf#page=126)

5. **Question:** Can the discussed interactive proofs use weaker provers than their counting-based implementations? — Chapter 8, prover complexity. [PDF p. 178](../pdf/arora_barak.pdf#page=178)

6. **Question:** Can all #P counting functions be computed in polynomial time? — §9.1. [PDF p. 188](../pdf/arora_barak.pdf#page=188)

7. **Question:** Would P=NP imply polynomial-time exact computation of every #P function? — §9.4. [PDF p. 202](../pdf/arora_barak.pdf#page=202)

8. **Conjecture:** Is deterministic communication polynomially bounded by logarithmic real matrix rank? — Conjecture 12.17. [PDF p. 243](../pdf/arora_barak.pdf#page=243)

9. **Question:** Construct explicit Boolean functions with strong general circuit lower bounds. — §16.1 discussion. [PDF p. 299](../pdf/arora_barak.pdf#page=299)

10. **Question:** Determine the relationships between BQP and classical classes, particularly NP and BPP. — §20.8. [PDF p. 445](../pdf/arora_barak.pdf#page=445)
