## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Research direction:** Scale compact join structures to many attributes, including hybrid worst-case-optimal and conventional query plans. — §6, higher dimensions. [PDF p. 13](../pdf/compact_databases.pdf#page=13)

2. **Research direction:** Efficiently combine graph patterns, regular paths, ranking, similarity joins and spatiotemporal predicates; define suitable optimality guarantees. — §6, functionality. [PDF p. 13](../pdf/compact_databases.pdf#page=13)

3. **Research direction:** Integrate compact structures into complete SPARQL-capable graph database engines. — §6, real systems. [PDF p. 13](../pdf/compact_databases.pdf#page=13)

4. **Research direction:** Support exact or approximate result counting without enumeration, and extend compact representations to analytical matrices. — §6, analytics. [PDF p. 14](../pdf/compact_databases.pdf#page=14)
