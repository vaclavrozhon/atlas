7. **Question:** Establish nearly quadratic adaptive versus nonadaptive testing gaps. — Open Problem 8.27. [PDF p. 223](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=223)

8. **Question:** Obtain the proposed cleaner local reductions from cycle-freeness to bipartiteness. — Open Problem 9.23. [PDF p. 266](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=266)

9. **Question:** Test minor-closed bounded-degree graph properties with polynomial dependence on degree and proximity. — Open Problem 9.26. [PDF p. 268](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=268)

10. **Question:** Relate ordinary and tolerant testing complexity for natural dense-graph property classes. — Open Problem 12.4. [PDF p. 366](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=366)

11. **Question:** Determine existence of linear-length locally testable codes and proofs. — Open Problem 13.12. [PDF p. 414](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=414)

12. **Question:** Separate lengths achievable by locally decodable and relaxed locally decodable codes. — §13.4 discussion. [PDF p. 423](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=423)

13. **Question:** Can completeness of the indistinguishability lower-bound method be proved without the threshold slack in the stated converse? — Exercise 7.3, footnote 31. [PDF p. 180](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=180)

14. **Question:** Find natural dense-graph properties testable with fewer than tower(log(1/ε)) queries but requiring superpolynomial dependence on 1/ε, for example exponential query complexity. — §8.5.1, intermediate complexity. [PDF p. 220](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=220)

15. **Conjecture:** For each t≥4, there are graph properties with nearly linear reciprocal-distance adaptive complexity and nonadaptive complexity Θ(ε^(−2+2/t)). — §8.5.3, conjecture following examples. [PDF p. 222](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=222)

16. **Conjecture:** Every nontrivial monotone property of k-vertex graphs requires Ω(k²) randomized adjacency queries for exact recognition. — §8.6.3, randomized graph-property recognition. [PDF p. 227](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=227)

17. **Research direction:** Complete the characterization of bounded-degree graph properties admitting constant-query one-sided proximity-oblivious testers, removing the stated combinatorial-conjecture dependency. — §9.7, proximity-oblivious testers. [PDF p. 280](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=280)

18. **Question:** Test acyclicity of bounded-degree digraphs using o(k) queries. — §9.7.2, directed acyclicity. [PDF p. 283](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=283)

19. **Question:** Does testing m-grained distributions themselves (δ=0), for m=Θ(n), require Ω(n/log n) samples? — Corollary 11.31, footnote 46; setup p.353. [PDF p. 354](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=354)

20. **Conjecture:** P differs from NP. — §12.6, motivating complexity conjecture. [PDF p. 377](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=377)

21. **Question:** Is graph nonisomorphism in BPP? — §13, footnote 28. [PDF p. 401](../pdf/goldreich2017.pdf#page=401)
