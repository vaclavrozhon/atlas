## Why included

A major reference for Boolean circuit lower-bound methods.

## Reading scope

Selected research problems from an early draft with more than forty marked problems.

This is a source inventory. The entries describe what this version presents as unresolved. Current open status has not been checked. Summaries can group related variants and are not substitutes for the source definitions.

This background reference retains the original selective inventory; it is not part of the specialist reread for the 35 categories.

The draft contains unresolved cross-references and repeated problem numbering; PDF pages disambiguate the entries.

## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Question:** Prove polynomial CNF-complexity lower bounds from average degree for bipartite K₂,₂-free graphs. — Research Problem 1.33; also 11.17. [PDF p. 51](../pdf/jukna2012.pdf#page=51)

2. **Question:** Exhibit graphs with superlogarithmic nondeterministic clique-versus-independent-set communication complexity. — Research Problem 4.15. [PDF p. 110](../pdf/jukna2012.pdf#page=110)

3. **Question:** Is formula size polynomially bounded by the stated monochromatic partition measure? — Research Problem 6.24. [PDF p. 183](../pdf/jukna2012.pdf#page=183)

4. **Question:** Separate polynomial-size monotone circuits from superpolynomial monotone span-program size. — Research Problem 8.7. [PDF p. 236](../pdf/jukna2012.pdf#page=236)

5. **Question:** Separate ordinary and monotone span-program size for monotone functions. — Research Problem 8.12. [PDF p. 239](../pdf/jukna2012.pdf#page=239)

6. **Question:** Strengthen monotone perfect-matching circuit lower bounds to stretched exponential. — Research Problem 9.39. [PDF p. 283](../pdf/jukna2012.pdf#page=283)

7. **Question:** Strengthen explicit depth-three Boolean circuit lower bounds beyond the stated barriers. — Research Problems 11.2 and 11.15. [PDF p. 306](../pdf/jukna2012.pdf#page=306)

8. **Question:** Prove exponential lower bounds for unrestricted depth-two threshold circuits. — Research Problem 11.40. [PDF p. 334](../pdf/jukna2012.pdf#page=334)

9. **Question:** Prove or refute that Majority lies outside ACC⁰. — Research Problem 12.28. [PDF p. 359](../pdf/jukna2012.pdf#page=359)

10. **Question:** Prove exponential lower bounds for weakly read-once nondeterministic branching programs. — Research Problem 16.12. [PDF p. 461](../pdf/jukna2012.pdf#page=461)
