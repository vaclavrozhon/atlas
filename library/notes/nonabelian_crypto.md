## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Question:** Which platform groups, finite or infinite, best support noncommutative cryptography? — §11 General 1–2. [PDF p. 27](../pdf/nonabelian_crypto.pdf#page=27)

2. **Research direction:** Prove protocol security and choose appropriate practical, worst-case, average-case or generic-case security measures. — §11 General 3,5–6. [PDF p. 27](../pdf/nonabelian_crypto.pdf#page=27)

3. **Research direction:** Construct public-key systems from further group-theoretic search and decision problems. — §11 General 4. [PDF p. 27](../pdf/nonabelian_crypto.pdf#page=27)

4. **Research direction:** Design additional noncommutative signatures and authentication schemes. — §11 General 7–8. [PDF p. 27](../pdf/nonabelian_crypto.pdf#page=27)

5. **Question:** Which commuting subgroups of polycyclic groups yield secure protocols? — §11 General 9. [PDF p. 27](../pdf/nonabelian_crypto.pdf#page=27)

6. **Question:** Determine polycyclic conjugacy-search complexity. — §11 Complexity 1. [PDF p. 27](../pdf/nonabelian_crypto.pdf#page=27)

7. **Question:** Determine polycyclic conjugacy-decision complexity, including the Eick–Ostheimer algorithm. — §11 Complexity 2. [PDF p. 27](../pdf/nonabelian_crypto.pdf#page=27)

8. **Question:** Determine collection-algorithm complexity for polycyclic normal forms. — §11 Complexity 3. [PDF p. 27](../pdf/nonabelian_crypto.pdf#page=27)

9. **Question:** Determine twisted conjugacy-search complexity in polycyclic groups. — §11 Complexity 4. [PDF p. 28](../pdf/nonabelian_crypto.pdf#page=28)

10. **Question:** Determine Reidemeister–Schreier rewriting complexity in free groups. — §11 Complexity 5. [PDF p. 28](../pdf/nonabelian_crypto.pdf#page=28)

11. **Question:** Determine generic-case polycyclic conjugacy-search complexity. — §11 Complexity 6. [PDF p. 28](../pdf/nonabelian_crypto.pdf#page=28)

12. **Question:** Can quantum algorithms accelerate polycyclic conjugacy search and other cryptographic decision problems? — §11 Quantum, introduction and 1. [PDF p. 28](../pdf/nonabelian_crypto.pdf#page=28)

13. **Research direction:** Implement the proposed systems securely and practically, including possible GAP-based implementations. — §11 Implementation 1–2. [PDF p. 28](../pdf/nonabelian_crypto.pdf#page=28)
