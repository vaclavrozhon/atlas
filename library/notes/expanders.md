## Why included

A landmark survey connecting expansion with combinatorics, algorithms and complexity.

## Reading scope

Selected numbered questions and conjectures in the 2006 author draft.

This is a source inventory. The entries describe what this version presents as unresolved. Current open status has not been checked. Summaries can group related variants and are not substitutes for the source definitions.

This background reference retains the original selective inventory; it is not part of the specialist reread for the 35 categories.

Conjecture 13.12 is explicitly refuted in the source and is excluded. Other entries retain their historical status.

## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Question:** Sharply control sparse k-vertex induced subgraphs in regular graphs, including girth-related extremal bounds. — Open Problem 5.1. [PDF p. 48](../pdf/expanders.pdf#page=48)

2. **Conjecture:** Do arbitrarily large d-regular Ramanujan graphs exist for every d≥3? — Conjecture 5.13. [PDF p. 53](../pdf/expanders.pdf#page=53)

3. **Conjecture:** Find two-lifts or adjacency signings with the stated two-sided Ramanujan spectral bounds. — Conjectures 6.8–6.10. [PDF p. 57](../pdf/expanders.pdf#page=57)

4. **Conjecture:** Are new eigenvalues of typical large lifts bounded by the universal-cover spectral radius plus o(1)? — Conjecture 7.8. [PDF p. 65](../pdf/expanders.pdf#page=65)

5. **Question:** Determine the probability that random lifts satisfy exact Ramanujan eigenvalue bounds. — Open Problem 7.11. [PDF p. 66](../pdf/expanders.pdf#page=66)

6. **Question:** Describe the coordinate distribution of nontrivial eigenvectors of random regular graphs. — Open Problem 7.12. [PDF p. 67](../pdf/expanders.pdf#page=67)

7. **Question:** Explicitly construct regular graphs with vertex expansion approaching d−2 on small sets. — Open Problem 10.8. [PDF p. 87](../pdf/expanders.pdf#page=87)

8. **Question:** Approximate the cut cone within constant distortion using a cone with efficient separation. — Open Problem 13.11. [PDF p. 113](../pdf/expanders.pdf#page=113)
