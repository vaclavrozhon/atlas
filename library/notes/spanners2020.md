## Why included

A broad reference on spanner constructions, extremal bounds and algorithmic variants.

## Reading scope

Selected questions from the dedicated open-problem sections; related construction directions are grouped.

This is a source inventory. The entries describe what this version presents as unresolved. Current open status has not been checked. Summaries can group related variants and are not substitutes for the source definitions.

This background reference retains the original selective inventory; it is not part of the specialist reread for the 35 categories.

The practical polynomial-size pairwise-spanner ILP question is already answered within the survey and is excluded.

## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Research direction:** Study spanners with discontinuous distortion functions. — §2.5, item 1. [PDF p. 11](../pdf/spanners2020.pdf#page=11)

2. **Conjecture:** Resolve the remaining cases of the Erdős girth conjecture. — §2.5, item 2. [PDF p. 11](../pdf/spanners2020.pdf#page=11)

3. **Question:** Classify minimum t-spanner hardness for the eight listed bounded-degree parameter pairs. — §3.5. [PDF p. 13](../pdf/spanners2020.pdf#page=13)

4. **Research direction:** Develop reverse-greedy and generalized greedy spanner constructions. — §4.7. [PDF p. 20](../pdf/spanners2020.pdf#page=20)

5. **Research direction:** Turn standard clustering methods into spanners with provable quality. — §5.5. [PDF p. 27](../pdf/spanners2020.pdf#page=27)

6. **Research direction:** Improve spanner sampling distributions and the Elkin–Neiman construction’s size guarantee. — §6.2. [PDF p. 29](../pdf/spanners2020.pdf#page=29)

7. **Question:** Maximize the pair count admitting linear-size pairwise spanners with constant additive error. — §7.5. [PDF p. 33](../pdf/spanners2020.pdf#page=33)

8. **Question:** Does every unweighted graph admit a +4 spanner with O(n^(4/3)) edges? — §8.4, item 1. [PDF p. 37](../pdf/spanners2020.pdf#page=37)

9. **Question:** Match Thorup–Zwick emulator tradeoffs using actual spanners. — §8.4, item 2. [PDF p. 37](../pdf/spanners2020.pdf#page=37)

10. **Question:** Determine optimal additive error for linear-size spanners. — §8.4, item 3. [PDF p. 37](../pdf/spanners2020.pdf#page=37)

11. **Question:** Construct O(n^(4/3))-edge weighted spanners with additive error 6W. — §8.4, item 4. [PDF p. 37](../pdf/spanners2020.pdf#page=37)

12. **Question:** Close the bounds for fault-tolerant sourcewise distance preservers. — §12.1. [PDF p. 55](../pdf/spanners2020.pdf#page=55)
