## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Question:** Prove superlinear unconditional SAT time lower bounds in robust unrestricted computation models; establish stronger exponential lower bounds. — §1, SAT lower bounds. [PDF p. 2](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=2)

2. **Conjecture:** P differs from NP. — §1, P versus NP. [PDF p. 2](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=2)

3. **Conjecture:** For every ε>0 some clause width k precludes randomized O(2^((1−ε)n)) time for k-SAT. — Hypothesis 1, SETH. [PDF p. 4](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=4)

4. **Conjecture:** Integer 3-SUM admits no randomized truly subquadratic algorithm. — Hypothesis 2, 3-SUM. [PDF p. 5](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=5)

5. **Conjecture:** Weighted APSP admits no randomized truly subcubic algorithm in the stated integer-weight regime. — Hypothesis 3, APSP. [PDF p. 6](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=6)

6. **Conjecture:** Boolean k-Orthogonal Vectors admits no randomized n^(k−ε) poly(d) algorithm for constant ε>0. — Hypothesis 4, k-OV. [PDF p. 7](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=7)

7. **Question:** Improve the nearly n^(3/2) running time for 3/2-approximate diameter on sparse graphs, ideally to linear time. — §3, diameter approximation. [PDF p. 9](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=9)

8. **Question:** Is tree edit distance fine-grained equivalent to APSP? — §4, tree edit distance. [PDF p. 14](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=14)

9. **Conjecture:** Hitting Set on Boolean vectors admits no randomized truly subquadratic poly(d)-factor algorithm. — Hypothesis 5, HS. [PDF p. 15](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=15)

10. **Question:** Does the Orthogonal Vectors hypothesis imply the Hitting Set hypothesis? — §5, relationship of hypotheses. [PDF p. 16](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=16)

11. **Conjecture:** The matrix-multiplication exponent ωk/3 for k-Clique, primarily for k divisible by three, cannot be improved by a constant in the exponent. — Hypothesis 6, k-Clique. [PDF p. 16](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=16)

12. **Conjecture:** Edge-weighted Min-Weight k-Clique requires randomized n^(k−o(1)) time in the specified weight range. — Hypothesis 7, Min-Weight k-Clique. [PDF p. 16](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=16)

13. **Conjecture:** Exact-weight k-Clique requires randomized n^(k−o(1)) time in the specified weight range. — Hypothesis 8, Exact k-Clique. [PDF p. 16](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=16)

14. **Question:** Determine fine-grained relationships between APSP and 3-SUM. — §5, comparison with 3-SUM. [PDF p. 16](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=16)

15. **Research direction:** Formalize and resolve the informal hypothesis that combinatorial Boolean matrix multiplication requires n^(3−o(1)) time. — §5, combinatorial BMM. [PDF p. 17](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=17)

16. **Conjecture:** Online Boolean matrix-vector multiplication for n queries requires randomized total time n^(3−o(1)). — Hypothesis 9, OMV. [PDF p. 17](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=17)

17. **Conjecture:** Refuting unsatisfiable k-CNF formulas for unbounded k requires nondeterministic 2^(n−o(n)) time. — Hypothesis 10, NSETH. [PDF p. 18](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=18)

18. **Conjecture:** Circuit-SAT for the described richer representation classes, especially polynomial-size polylog-depth circuits, requires 2^(n−o(n)) time. — §6, C-SETH and NC-SETH. [PDF p. 18](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=18)

19. **Conjecture:** At least one of SETH, the APSP hypothesis and the 3-SUM hypothesis is true. — §6, disjunction of hypotheses. [PDF p. 19](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=19)

20. **Question:** Construct further cryptographic primitives, including one-way functions, from fine-grained assumptions. — §7, fine-grained cryptography. [PDF p. 20](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=20)

21. **Research direction:** Prove strong unconditional lower bounds for suitably space-restricted 3-SUM algorithms. — §7, algorithmic time–space tradeoffs. [PDF p. 20](../pdf/fine_grained.pdf#page=20)
