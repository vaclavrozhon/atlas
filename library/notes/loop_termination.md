## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Question:** Decide whether an integer linear recurrence ever equals zero, including the unresolved order-five case. — §1, Skolem Problem; also §4. [PDF p. 2](../pdf/loop_termination.pdf#page=2)

2. **Question:** Decide nonnegativity of every term; unresolved cases include general order six and simple orders above nine. — §1, Positivity. [PDF p. 2](../pdf/loop_termination.pdf#page=2)

3. **Question:** Decide eventual nonnegativity for arbitrary linear recurrences, already unresolved at order six. — §1, Ultimate Positivity. [PDF p. 2](../pdf/loop_termination.pdf#page=2)

4. **Question:** Compute eventual-positivity thresholds for simple recurrences beyond the established low-order cases. — §1, effective threshold. [PDF p. 3](../pdf/loop_termination.pdf#page=3)

5. **Research direction:** Make the relevant transcendental Diophantine-approximation bounds effective enough for recurrence decision procedures. — §1 and §2, Diophantine barriers. [PDF p. 3](../pdf/loop_termination.pdf#page=3)

6. **Question:** Decide termination over all integer starting states for general affine linear loops with nondiagonalizable updates. — §3 and §4, integer loops. [PDF p. 6](../pdf/loop_termination.pdf#page=6)

7. **Question:** Decide termination for loops whose updates are general linear constraints between successive states. — §4, linear constraint loops. [PDF p. 8](../pdf/loop_termination.pdf#page=8)
