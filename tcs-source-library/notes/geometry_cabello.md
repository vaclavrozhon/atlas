## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Question:** Is expected bounding-box volume FPT in dimension, or is its decision version W[1]-hard? — §2, stochastic bounding box. [PDF p. 3](../pdf/geometry_cabello.pdf#page=3)

2. **Research direction:** Find approximation schemes for expected bounding-box volume with mild dimension dependence. — §2, approximation. [PDF p. 3](../pdf/geometry_cabello.pdf#page=3)

3. **Question:** Compute maximum matchings of unit-disk or unit-square intersection graphs in near-linear time. — §3, uncolored intersections. [PDF p. 3](../pdf/geometry_cabello.pdf#page=3)

4. **Question:** Obtain near-linear maximum matching for bichromatic unit disks or squares. — §3, bichromatic intersections. [PDF p. 4](../pdf/geometry_cabello.pdf#page=4)

5. **Question:** Can the corresponding matching-existence test be faster than constructing the matching? — §3, recognition variant. [PDF p. 4](../pdf/geometry_cabello.pdf#page=4)

6. **Question:** Determine the complexity of barrier resilience for disks, including unit disks, in an annulus. — §4, annular domain. [PDF p. 5](../pdf/geometry_cabello.pdf#page=5)

7. **Question:** Is minimum total barrier shrinkage polynomial-time solvable or NP-hard in a rectangle? — §4, shrinking; Figure 4. [PDF p. 5](../pdf/geometry_cabello.pdf#page=5)
