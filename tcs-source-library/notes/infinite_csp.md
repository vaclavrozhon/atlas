## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Research direction:** Identify natural subclasses of NP exhibiting a P/NP-complete dichotomy. — Question 1. [PDF p. 14](../pdf/infinite_csp.pdf#page=14)

2. **Conjecture:** Every finite-template CSP is polynomial-time solvable or NP-complete. — §6, finite-template dichotomy. [PDF p. 14](../pdf/infinite_csp.pdf#page=14)

3. **Question:** Is every NP problem polynomial-time equivalent to an ω-categorical-template CSP? — Question 2. [PDF p. 15](../pdf/infinite_csp.pdf#page=15)

4. **Question:** Can every CSP in SNP be represented by an ω-categorical template? — Question 3; §12.3. [PDF p. 15](../pdf/infinite_csp.pdf#page=15)

5. **Question:** Is the algebraic NP-hardness criterion of Theorem 10 necessary for ω-categorical-template CSPs? — Question 4. [PDF p. 20](../pdf/infinite_csp.pdf#page=20)

6. **Research direction:** Classify arbitrary-arity temporal, interval, partial/branching-time, RCC-5 and related qualitative CSP languages. — §12.1. [PDF p. 26](../pdf/infinite_csp.pdf#page=26)

7. **Question:** When can varieties replace pseudovarieties in capturing infinite-template CSP complexity? — §12.2(a). [PDF p. 26](../pdf/infinite_csp.pdf#page=26)

8. **Research direction:** Develop tame congruence theory for oligomorphic algebras. — §12.2(b). [PDF p. 27](../pdf/infinite_csp.pdf#page=27)

9. **Question:** Lift complexity classifications from first-order definitions to first-order interpretations. — §12.2(c). [PDF p. 27](../pdf/infinite_csp.pdf#page=27)

10. **Research direction:** Use model-theoretic and Jordan-group classifications to classify associated CSPs. — §12.2(d). [PDF p. 27](../pdf/infinite_csp.pdf#page=27)

11. **Question:** Which CSPs in NP are definable in SNP? — §12.3, final question. [PDF p. 27](../pdf/infinite_csp.pdf#page=27)
