## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Conjecture:** Every synchronizing n-state automaton admits a reset word of length at most (n−1)². — Conjecture 1. [PDF p. 2](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=2)

2. **Conjecture:** The state-avoiding threshold is at most 2n−2, under the source’s avoidability convention. — Conjecture 2. [PDF p. 3](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=3)

3. **Question:** Find tight avoid-or-compress bounds depending on subset sizes, including strongly connected synchronizing automata. — Open Problem 4. [PDF p. 4](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=4)

4. **Conjecture:** Avoid any k<n states of a synchronizing automaton within O(kn) letters. — Conjecture 5. [PDF p. 4](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=4)

5. **Conjecture:** Compress any specified state with another within O(n) letters in strongly connected synchronizing automata. — Conjecture 6. [PDF p. 4](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=4)

6. **Conjecture:** Verify synchronization and the claimed 4n/3 compression threshold of the explicit A_k family. — Conjecture 8. [PDF p. 5](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=5)

7. **Conjecture:** Prove Cardoso’s stated bound for synchronizing a specified subset. — Conjecture 9. [PDF p. 6](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=6)

8. **Conjecture:** Some k-state subset synchronizes within O(kn) letters. — Conjecture 11. [PDF p. 6](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=6)

9. **Question:** Bound words synchronizing a prescribed subset together with k additional states. — Open Problem 12. [PDF p. 6](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=6)

10. **Question:** Beat quadratic-time synchronizability testing over a fixed alphabet, or establish conditional barriers. — Open Problem 13. [PDF p. 7](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=7)

11. **Conjecture:** Random binary automata have reset threshold Θ(√n) with high probability. — Conjecture 16. [PDF p. 8](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=8)

12. **Conjecture:** Random binary automata have expected reset threshold Θ(√n). — Conjecture 17. [PDF p. 9](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=9)

13. **Conjecture:** Is the Eulerian reset-threshold lower bound ⌊(n²−3)/2⌋ tight? — §2.8.2, unnumbered conjecture. [PDF p. 11](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=11)

14. **Question:** Determine tight initial-subspace dimension bounds for iterative algebraic synchronization methods. — Open Problem 22. [PDF p. 13](../pdf/sync_automata.pdf#page=13)
