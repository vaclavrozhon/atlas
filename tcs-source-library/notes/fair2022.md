## Open questions and directions in the source

1. **Question:** Compute an EF1 and Pareto-optimal allocation in polynomial time. — Open Problem 1. [PDF p. 4](../pdf/fair2022.pdf#page=4)

2. **Question:** Do EFX allocations exist for at least four agents with unrestricted additive valuations? — Open Problem 2. [PDF p. 5](../pdf/fair2022.pdf#page=5)

3. **Question:** Determine the best universally guaranteed multiplicative EFX approximation. — Open Problem 3. [PDF p. 5](../pdf/fair2022.pdf#page=5)

4. **Question:** Achieve exact EFX while donating only a sublinear number of goods. — Open Problem 4. [PDF p. 6](../pdf/fair2022.pdf#page=6)

5. **Question:** Improve the stated additive-valuation maximin-share approximation and impossibility bounds. — Open Problem 5. [PDF p. 7](../pdf/fair2022.pdf#page=7)

6. **Research direction:** Identify further structured valuation classes guaranteeing exact maximin-share allocations. — Open Problem 6. [PDF p. 7](../pdf/fair2022.pdf#page=7)

7. **Question:** Does envy-freeness up to a random good always exist? — Open Problem 7. [PDF p. 7](../pdf/fair2022.pdf#page=7)

8. **Question:** Do pairwise maximin-share allocations always exist? — Open Problem 8. [PDF p. 8](../pdf/fair2022.pdf#page=8)

9. **Question:** Determine the best universal approximation to groupwise maximin-share fairness. — Open Problem 9. [PDF p. 8](../pdf/fair2022.pdf#page=8)

10. **Question:** Design mechanisms whose pure Nash equilibria guarantee fairness stronger than EF1. — Open Problem 10. [PDF p. 9](../pdf/fair2022.pdf#page=9)
