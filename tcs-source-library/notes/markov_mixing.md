12. **Question:** Can the c_(Δ,q) n log n lower bound for coloring Glauber dynamics use a universal positive constant independent of Δ and q? — Chapter 7 notes, coloring lower bound. [PDF p. 115](../pdf/markov_mixing.pdf#page=115)

13. **Question:** Can the exact cover time of a finite graph be computed deterministically in polynomial time? — Chapter 11 notes, computing cover time. [PDF p. 175](../pdf/markov_mixing.pdf#page=175)

14. **Conjecture:** For L≈n^α with 0<α<1, the L-reversal shuffle mixes in Θ(n^max(1,3−3α) log n) time, as conjectured by Durrett. — Chapter 16 notes; continues p.258. [PDF p. 257](../pdf/markov_mixing.pdf#page=257)
