# Selected mathematical relationship checks

The card statements and their saved sources are the main evidence for relatedness.
These additional primary-source checks support selected clusters; they are not
an exhaustive verification of every source or a new current-status review.

- **One-way functions and P versus NP:** Goldreich explicitly records that
  P=NP rules out one-way functions. The relation is symmetric in navigation;
  the logical implication is one-directional. The converse from P≠NP is itself
  a separate problem. [Foundations of Cryptography reading guide](https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~oded/foc-gl.html).
- **Public-key encryption from one-wayness:** a public-key construction from
  arbitrary one-way functions would connect the general primitive-existence
  questions, while black-box barriers must not be mistaken for unrestricted
  impossibility. [Applebaum, Barak and Wigderson, Public-Key Cryptography from Different Assumptions](https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~bennyap/pubs/ncpkcFull1.pdf).
- **Compiled Boolean representations:** succinctness and support for efficient
  transformations are distinct dimensions. A polynomial-time negation algorithm
  provides polynomial-size output; merely proving that such output exists does
  not supply an algorithm. The existing TCS-0306/TCS-7260 directional metadata
  remains intact. [Darwiche and Marquis, A Knowledge Compilation Map](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/afs/cs.cmu.edu/project/jair/pub/volume17/darwiche02a.pdf).
- **Recurrences and termination:** Skolem, Positivity and Ultimate Positivity are
  closely related but different decision questions. Their connection to linear
  loop termination supports cross-category navigation. [Ouaknine and Worrell, On linear recurrence sequences and loop termination](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/publications/lrs-survey15abs.html),
  [Bacik et al., 2026 survey](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/publications/skolem_and_positivity_survey26abs.html).
- **Fine-grained reductions:** SETH and orthogonal-vector problems are linked by
  explicit reductions; time exponents, the vector dimension, field and randomized
  versus deterministic conventions matter. Related navigation does not assert
  equivalence of the separate conjectures. [Williams and Yu, Finding orthogonal vectors in discrete structures](https://people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/orthog-soda.pdf).

Many remaining accepted links are editorial structural comparisons, for example
variants of the same coding or graph-query target. They assert useful proximity,
not an unrecorded theorem or a suggested solution method.
