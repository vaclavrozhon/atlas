The five broader matches are explicitly recorded as coverage rather than exact equivalence. Archived matches retain their previous selection decisions. Some literature links remain bibliographic pointers. This import does not certify a complete literature review, full benchmark readiness, or exhaustive coverage of Wikipedia.

## Status and formulation corrections

- WIKI-112 was excluded: necessary envy-freeness is NP-complete already for three agents, with hardness for every fixed number at least three. [Aziz, Schlotter and Walsh, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mathsocsci.2023.08.002).
- WIKI-080 was mapped to TCS-1538: the stack-to-queue and mixed-to-queue boundedness questions are equivalent. [Katheder et al., STACS 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.17776).
- WIKI-087 retains polynomial dependence on maximum codeword length. The weaker bound in total codeword length is already known. [Kiefer and Mascle, STACS 2019](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2019.43).
- WIKI-126 originally retained classification of when FIFO feedback helps, since examples of a strict capacity increase are already known. That research-direction card was subsequently deleted at the user's request. [Aptel and Tchamkerten, 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.06190).
- WIKI-022 adds p-optimal proof systems; the ordinary-optimality discussion is already represented in TCS-5292. WIKI-103 separates polynomial-time EFX approximation from its existence guarantee. WIKI-123 fixes a discrete memoryless interference channel, distinct from the existing Gaussian-channel card.
