        Practical Considerations
Public-key encryption is orders of magnitude slower
than secret-key encryption.

1. We mostly showed (except El Gamal) how to
   encrypt bit-by-bit! Super-duper inefficient.
2. Exponentiation takes 𝑂(𝑛" ) time as opposed to
   typically linear time for secret key encryption (AES).
3. The 𝑛 itself is large for PKE (RSA: 𝑛 ≥ 2048)
   compared to SKE (AES: 𝑛 = 128).
                           (For Elliptic Curve El-Gamal, it’s 320 bits)

Can solve problem 1 and minimize problems 2&3 using
hybrid encryption.
