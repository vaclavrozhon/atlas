# Research notes and sources

This directory retains research conclusions, dated reviews and source documents.
Current problem content lives in [data/cards/](../data/cards/); current scientific
rules live in [docs/RULES.md](../docs/RULES.md). Research notes provide provenance and discussion,
not an additional publication layer.

The repository cleanup removed historical catalogue snapshots, restoration
archives, extracted candidate batches, replay scripts, logs and browser captures.
Full archived-card copies in retained research audits were reduced to IDs and
removal reasons. Historical reports may describe counts or workflows from their
original date; they do not override the current data layout or activity policy.
The 13 September 2026 decision now preserves inactive cards in
`data/archive/cards/`. Routine research and improvements target `data/cards/`
only; the archive is outside the research backlog unless explicitly requested.
