# Research notes and sources

This directory retains research conclusions, dated reviews and source documents.
Current problem content lives in [data/cards/](../data/cards/); current scientific
rules live in [docs/RULES.md](../docs/RULES.md). Research notes provide provenance and discussion,
not an additional publication layer.

The repository cleanup removed historical catalogue snapshots, restoration
archives, extracted candidate batches, replay scripts, logs and browser captures.
Full archived-card copies in retained research audits were reduced to IDs and
removal reasons. Historical reports may describe counts or workflows from their
original date; they do not override the current data layout or deletion policy.

- [review-notes.md](review-notes.md): preserved model distinctions and dated source checks.
- [pruning-50-and-merges-20260912/](pruning-50-and-merges-20260912/README.md): applied 50 removals and three duplicate consolidations from the 100-card proposal; the other 47 candidates were retained unchanged.
- [pruning-proposal-100-20260912/](pruning-proposal-100-20260912/README.md): 100 proposed removals after screening all 1,066 active cards; individual reasons, source checks and conditional consolidations, with no deletions applied.
- [quantitative-questions-20260912/](quantitative-questions-20260912/README.md): numerical/function reformulations, six authorized restorations and Lean accuracy policy.
- [interest-pruning-20260911/](interest-pruning-20260911/README.md): first 300 individually justified removals toward approximately 800 candidates, including early-ranked cards.
- `sources/`: 523 paper metadata/text files referenced by retained cards. Their
  original-to-current paths are recorded in `sources/path-mapping.json`.
- `continuation/` and dated source directories: source documents and readable text.
- Dated review directories: scientific conclusions, source inventories and decisions.
- Reviews explicitly marked in progress retain their working files while active.

The independent book and survey library is in [library/](../library/README.md).
Neither directory is needed to publish the reader or run the offline code checks.
