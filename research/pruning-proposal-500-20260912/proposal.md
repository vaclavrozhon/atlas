Reviewed all 1558 currently active records. This is a proposal only; no catalogue cards were removed. Accepting this batch would leave 1058 active records, so it does not by itself finish selection of Top500.

Independent scientific importance first; overlap second. No category quotas, score cutoffs, or formalization-cost filter.

Every active card was displayed and editorially assessed using its question or source-grounded summary and motivation. Initial candidates received a second pass over remaining summary sentences and substantive significance notes; selected borderline cases received fuller record and primary-source checks. This is not a fresh full-paper or current-status certification of every card.

Numerical and function-valued targets are valid; lack of a simple closed form or difficulty of Lean formalization is not a removal reason.

The first pass produced 528 candidates. The second pass retained 26 on substantive importance grounds; 2 other candidates had been marked resolved by concurrent catalogue work and are not counted among these 500.

The per-card basis labels distinguish scientific selection, secondary consolidation, and questions already answered or rejected in their source. Consolidation proposals require preserving relevant references and checking any explicitly noted model conventions before implementation. No such implementation is performed here.
