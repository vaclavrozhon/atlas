## Remaining formulation choices

- **TCS-0318 — How many k-element subsets can a line separate from a planar point set?:** K₂(n,k) and the uniform constant-factor target are defined, but the admissible representation of the answer F(n,k) is not. Allowing arbitrary functions admits F=K₂ itself, while an arbitrarily imposed expression grammar or computational bound would change the question. An answer representation must be selected before this is a closed benchmark specification.
- **TCS-0679 — Linear conditional information for proper agnostic VC learning:** The arbitrary-domain source leaves sigma algebras, measurability of evaluation and admissibility of learners/distributions unspecified. The record now distinguishes Conjecture 6 from the refuted Conjectures 7 and 8. Restricting to finite/countable domains or imposing a measurable-selection hypothesis would be a substantive specialization still to be settled.
- **TCS-6606 — The exact capacity region of the two-user Gaussian interference channel:** The operational capacity region and supporting function are defined. The accepted finite-dimensional characterization has no specified syntax, allowed auxiliary distributions, dimension bound or evaluation requirement. Excluding a restatement informally is not a mathematical acceptance predicate; no new representation restriction is invented here.
- **TCS-1010 — The optimal asymptotic binary rate–distance tradeoff:** The target R₂(δ) and equality conditions are defined, but an admissible answer representation is not. An arbitrary formula can just restate the defining optimization and limsup. A fixed representation or a separately approved proposition is still needed; replacing the question by tightness of a particular coding bound would change it.

## Source corrections and interpretations

- TCS-0679: [Haghifam et al., Theorem 4.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.02970) refutes the proper realizable Conjectures 7 and 8. The record now singles out Conjecture 6 of the [original paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v125/steinke20b.html), with its measurable-space gap retained.
- TCS-1033: the previous formal statement and answer criterion described different claims. The title’s polynomial-testability implication is now the sole target; the stronger quantitative conversion of [Problem 5.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.16878v1) is recorded as distinct.
- TCS-6554: the slack constant is existentially quantified before the fixed degree; the source’s [low-degree caveat](https://arxiv.org/html/1705.04840) is handled by the factor 1/2.
- TCS-0011: the positive-good EFX convention and complete allocation follow [Definition 5 and Open Problem 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07551).
- TCS-6578: [Smale’s original Problem 7](https://www.fim.uni-passau.de/fileadmin/dokumente/fakultaeten/fim/lehrstuhl/muller/SmaleProblems1998.pdf) fixes real-number computation; the card does not replace it with a bit model.
- TCS-6631: probability kernels, replacement adjacency and expected normalized Euclidean error implement [Open Problem 2](https://differentialprivacy.org/open-problem-optimal-query-release/).
